150 Years Historical Timeline

Tracing Ongole from ancient historical roots through the formation of Prakasam District to the 2026 Sesquicentennial.

Ancient & Medieval Roots

Early historical references, followed by periods under Chola, Pallava, and Vijayanagara dynasties.

September 30, 1876

Ongole Municipality was officially constituted under British rule.

1920 & 1928

Active participation in the Non-Cooperation Movement and Simon Commission protests led by Tanguturi Prakasam.

February 2, 1970 & 1972

Ongole District created, later renamed Prakasam District in honor of Tanguturi Prakasam Panthulu.

2022 – Municipal Corporation

Upgraded to a Municipal Corporation status.

2026 – 150 Years Celebration

Commemorating 150 glorious years of civic governance from 1876 to 2026.
