Freedom Fighters & District Founders

Honoring the courageous leaders, freedom fighters, and visionaries who shaped Ongole and Prakasam District.

Tanguturi Prakasam Pantulu

"Andhra Kesari" / First CM of Andhra

Renowned for baring his chest before British guns during the 1928 Simon Commission protests in Madras and leading freedom struggles.

Unnava Lakshminarayana

Social Reformer & Writer

Freedom fighter and author of *Malapalli*, who fought relentlessly against untouchability and British oppression.

Duggirala Gopalakrishnaiah

"Andhra Ratna"

Spearheaded the historic Chirala-Perala tax-resistance movement and organized the 'Ramana Dandu' volunteer corps.

Konda Venkatappaiah

"Andhra Pathikudu"

Prominent freedom fighter, lawyer, and statesman from the region who played a foundational role in the Congress movement in Andhra.

Karavadi Venkateswarlu

Local Freedom Leader

Organized grassroots nationalist movements, mobilised local youth, and led satyagrahas across the Ongole region.

Prakasam District Founders

Civic & Administrative Visionaries

Leaders and committees who championed the official carving out of Ongole district in 1970, later renamed Prakasam District to honor Tanguturi Prakasam.
