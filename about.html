About Ongole 150 Years Committee

Dedicated to preserving and celebrating the rich history of Ongole Municipal Corporation.

The Ongole 150 Years Sesquicentennial Celebrations committee brings together historians, civic leaders, and citizens to commemorate 150 years of municipal governance from 1876 to 2026.

This digital portal serves as a permanent archive, timeline guide, and interactive gallery for citizens worldwide.
